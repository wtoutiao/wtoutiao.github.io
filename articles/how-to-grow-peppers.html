How to Grow Peppers: From Seed to Harvest

Updated October 2026 · 6 min read

Peppers are rewarding to grow and come in endless colors and heat levels. Whether you love sweet bells or fiery chiles, here's how to grow them from seed to harvest.

Starting Seeds Indoors

Peppers need a long warm season, so start seeds indoors 8-10 weeks before the last frost. Plant seeds 1/4 inch deep in seed-starting mix, keep them warm (75-80°F), and provide strong light once they sprout.

Hardening Off and Transplanting

About a week before transplanting, gradually expose seedlings to outdoor conditions. Then plant them in rich, well-drained soil in full sun (6-8 hours), 18 inches apart. Bury them up to the first true leaves.

Care

Peppers like consistent moisture but not soggy soil. Water at the base and mulch to retain warmth and moisture. Feed with a balanced fertilizer at planting, then a low-nitrogen feed once flowers form.

Harvesting

Sweet peppers can be picked green, but leaving them longer gives richer color and sweetness. Hot peppers are best fully colored for maximum heat. Use scissors to cut peppers, leaving a short stem attached.
