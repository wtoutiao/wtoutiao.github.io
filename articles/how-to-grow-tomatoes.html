How to Grow Tomatoes at Home: A Complete Guide

Updated October 2026 · 6 min read

Tomatoes are the most popular home-grown crop for a reason: they're delicious, productive, and surprisingly easy once you know a few basics. Here's everything you need to grow great tomatoes at home, whether you have a garden bed or just a sunny balcony.

Choose the Right Tomato Variety

Start with varieties suited to your space. Determinate types (like Roma and Bush Early Girl) stay compact and ripen all at once — great for containers and sauce. Indeterminate types (like Beefsteak and Cherry) keep growing and produce over a long season.

Planting

Tomatoes need 6-8 hours of sun and rich, well-drained soil. Plant them deep — bury the stem up to the first leaves, as roots form along the buried stem. In containers, use at least a 5-gallon pot.

Watering

Water deeply and consistently. Keep soil evenly moist but never soggy. Water at the base of the plant in the morning to avoid leaf diseases. Mulching helps hold moisture.

Staking and Support

Tomatoes grow better with support. Use a cage, stake, or trellis. Tie stems loosely so they don't snap. Prune off suckers in the leaf axils to channel energy into fruit.

Fertilizing

Feed with a balanced fertilizer at planting, then switch to a tomato-specific, higher-potassium feed once flowers appear. Avoid too much nitrogen, which makes leaves at the expense of fruit.

Harvesting

Pick tomatoes when fully colored and slightly soft. Harvest regularly to encourage more fruit. At the end of the season, green tomatoes will ripen indoors on a sunny windowsill.
