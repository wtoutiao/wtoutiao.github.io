10 Easy Flowers for Beginner Gardeners

Updated October 2026 · 6 min read

Not sure which flowers to plant? These ten are forgiving, colorful, and bloom with minimal effort — ideal for your first flower garden.

1. Marigolds

Sun-loving, pest-deterring, and blooming all season from direct-sown seed. Marigolds are nearly foolproof.

2. Sunflowers

Tall, cheerful, and easy from seed. Choose dwarf varieties for small spaces or containers.

3. Zinnias

Brilliant colors and long vase life. They thrive in heat and need only sun and water.

4. Cosmos

Delicate, airy blooms that self-sow. Cosmos are drought-tolerant and bloom for months.

5. Nasturtiums

Edible, trailing, and bright. They grow in poor soil and deter pests from vegetables.

6-10: More Easy Blooms

Petunias for continuous color in pots; geraniums for heat and dryness; pansies for cool spring; lavender for fragrance and pollinators; black-eyed Susan for tough, dependable perennials.

Tips for Flower Success

Plant in well-drained soil with 6 hours of sun, water at the base, and deadhead spent blooms to encourage more flowers. Start with these easy picks and your first garden will be a success.

Want to mix flowers with food? Pair them with our easy vegetable crops to attract pollinators.
