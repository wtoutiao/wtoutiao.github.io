Garden Pests: Natural Ways to Get Rid of Them

Updated October 2026 · 6 min read

You don't need harsh chemicals to keep your garden healthy. Most common pests have simple, natural remedies that work well and keep your food safe to eat.

Aphids

These tiny sap-suckers cluster on new growth. Blast them off with a strong stream of water, or spray with a mild soap solution (a few drops of dish soap in water). Encourage ladybugs, which eat aphids.

Slugs and Snails

They feast on young leaves at night. Remove hiding spots like boards and debris, handpick after dusk, and use barriers of crushed eggshells or diatomaceous earth. Beer traps work surprisingly well.

Caterpillars

Check leaves regularly and remove caterpillars by hand. Use floating row covers to keep butterflies from laying eggs. Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt) is an organic, targeted control.

Whiteflies and Spider Mites

Both thrive in dry conditions. Keep plants well-watered and mist leaves. Yellow sticky traps catch whiteflies; a strong spray and neem oil help with mites.

Prevention Is Best

  • Plant pest-resistant varieties.
  • Rotate crops each year.
  • Keep plants healthy — strong plants resist pests.
  • Invite beneficial insects with flowers like marigolds and dill.
