About BackyardGardenHub

Practical gardening for everyone

BackyardGardenHub is a free resource for anyone who wants to grow their own food — whether you have a backyard, a balcony, or just a sunny windowsill.

What We Do

We publish clear, step-by-step gardening guides on vegetables, herbs, berries, flowers, and soil. Every guide is written in plain language and focuses on practical methods that work for beginners, not garden jargon.

Our Philosophy

Growing food should be simple and rewarding. We believe you don't need a huge garden, expensive tools, or years of experience to grow something delicious. Start small, learn the basics, and expand from there.

Why We Exist

We're here to help you get real results — fresh tomatoes, crisp lettuce, and fragrant herbs from your own space. We keep our content free and supported by advertising, so you can learn without barriers.

Have a question or topic you'd like us to cover? Get in touch via our Contact page.
